Secure Socket Layer (SSL)

SSL (Secure Socket Layer) is a security protocol that protects the data exchanged between a user's browser and a website. It encrypts sensitive information such as passwords, personal details, and payment information, making it difficult for hackers to access the data.

Benefits of SSL

  • Encrypts customer information.
  • Protects online payment transactions.
  • Prevents data theft and cyber attacks.
  • Builds trust by displaying HTTPS in the browser.
  • Keeps personal and financial information secure.

Why SSL is Important for Brew Bliss Café

Brew Bliss Café provides online payment facilities for its customers. SSL ensures that all payment details and personal information remain safe and secure during online transactions, giving customers confidence while ordering from our website.
